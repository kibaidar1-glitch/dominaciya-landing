
# PROJECT — ДОМИНАЦИЯ

## Product
ДОМИНАЦИЯ — авторская настольная карточная игра
с тематикой античности, философии, власти и борьбы народов.

Игра существует в формате Print & Play:
игроки могут скачать материалы, распечатать компоненты
и подготовить игру самостоятельно.

## Website goal
Create a memorable product landing page that:
1. Explains the game's concept quickly.
2. Makes visitors interested in playing.
3. Shows the actual cards and game components.
4. Explains the core gameplay and key mechanics.
5. Leads visitors to the Print & Play download or purchase
   page, depending on the actual distribution model.

## Audience
- People interested in modern tabletop games.
- Fans of strategy, negotiation and competitive card games.
- Players who enjoy philosophy and ancient-history aesthetics.
- Print & Play enthusiasts.

## Brand personality
Intellectual, ambitious, strategic, austere, timeless.

## Core message
Создай новый мир. Возвысь свой народ.

## Product facts
- Player count: 2–9.
- Play time: 20–40 minutes.
- Age: 12+.

Confirm these details against the final game materials
before publication.

## Constraints
- Do not invent rules or components.
- Do not claim the game is free unless confirmed.
- Do not assume payment, email signup, or account features.
- The actual distribution method and download URL must be configured
  before the primary CTA is considered complete.
