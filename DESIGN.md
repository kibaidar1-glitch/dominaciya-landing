
# DESIGN — Art Direction

## Concept
Ancient Greek civilization interpreted through a minimalist,
editorial and philosophical design language.

The page should feel like an artifact from an ancient civilization
presented by a contemporary art director.

## Visual principles
- Strong typography and deliberate composition.
- Generous negative space.
- Restrained color palette.
- Large, carefully cropped artwork.
- Asymmetric layouts where they improve the composition.
- Subtle paper, stone and brush-stroke textures.
- Clear visual hierarchy and confident typography.

## Palette
Use these as initial design tokens, then refine against
the actual game artwork.

- Background: warm parchment / sand.
- Primary text: charcoal / near-black.
- Secondary surfaces: muted limestone.
- Accent: muted terracotta or antique bronze.
- Optional secondary accent: desaturated deep purple.

Do not use every accent everywhere.

## Typography
- Choose a distinctive display serif for major headings.
- Use a highly readable font for body text and controls.
- Keep line lengths comfortable.
- Avoid excessive uppercase text in long paragraphs.

## Artwork
- Prioritize original ДОМИНАЦИЯ card illustrations and packaging.
- Artwork should retain its original aspect ratio where possible.
- Avoid unnecessary filters that distort the original illustrations.
- Statues, ancient objects and monumental architecture are preferred
  over generic fantasy characters.
- Avoid modern-looking characters and unrelated stock photography.

## Components
- Restrained navigation.
- Strong hero section with one primary CTA.
- Editorial section layouts.
- Card gallery with consistent spacing.
- Clear feature blocks.
- High-contrast buttons with visible hover and focus states.
- Responsive navigation and layouts.

## Motion
Use subtle transitions only when they support hierarchy or feedback.
Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Avoid
- Generic startup or SaaS visual language.
- Neon colors and oversaturated gradients.
- Excessive rounded cards.
- Unnecessary icon collections.
- Decorative elements that compete with game artwork.
- Text baked into images when it should be real HTML text.

## Responsive behavior
Design deliberately for mobile, tablet and desktop.
Do not simply shrink the desktop layout.
